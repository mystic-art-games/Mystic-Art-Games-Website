About Helianthe

Helianthe is a competitive LCG about rebuilding a shared world.
Two Patrons shape the future through different visions.

The board begins almost empty. Over successive Seasons, habitats, places, characters, and events appear — and the traces of those decisions become a map of the entire game.

Vision & ideology

Helianthe is a game about a future built together with the living world, not at its expense. Every match should leave behind a readable landscape: where the first forest grew, which settlement became dependent on solar energy, and which fields survived through cooperation.

The game does not treat nature and technology as opposites. Symbiosis, flowing water, community renewal, and managed energy are different paths toward regeneration. Victory belongs to the vision that becomes most visible on the shared board.

Design philosophy

  • One board, many visions — players share the geography and change it for different purposes.
  • Cards are changes, not units — every card describes an influence on the world.
  • Spatial decisions matter more than combat arithmetic.
  • Regeneration replaces extraction as the primary language of development.
  • The world reacts — neighboring Fields and the evolving board state matter.

Rules at a glance

There are four Terrain types: Flow, Radiance, Symbiosis, and Verdant. Each player chooses a Patron and builds their deck around that Patron’s faction and vision.

Patron visions

  • Verdant: fill the entire board with Terrain, with no Verdant Terrain affected by Crisis.
  • Radiance: collect 20 Lumens on Radiance Terrains.
  • Symbiosis: achieve 5 Entanglements — connections between Symbiosis Terrain and other Terrain.
  • Flow: place 10 Flow Entities on any Terrain.

Core rules

  1. The game continues until one or both players fulfil their vision.
  2. The shared board has 19 Nodes. Players take turns, and each player draws one card at the beginning of their turn.
  3. The game uses two card types: Events and Entities.
  4. You may play any number of Events during your turn. Each Event is a one-time effect and goes straight to the Archive after resolving.
  5. Play an Entity by placing it on a matching Terrain on the map. Alternatively, archive the Entity instead of activating its effect to create its matching Terrain and place that Terrain on the map.
